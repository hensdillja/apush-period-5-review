BIG HISTORICAL QUESTION

How did expansion, the Civil War, and Reconstruction transform the United States from 1844–1877?

Think about causation (why the Civil War happened), comparison (Union vs. Confederacy; Reconstruction plans), and continuity and change (the meaning and limits of freedom after emancipation).

Six big questions to prepare for

  1. What were the consequences of Manifest Destiny for the United States?
  2. What political, economic, and social divisions caused the Civil War?
  3. Why did the Union ultimately win the war?
  4. How did the war affect civilians in the North and South?
  5. How did Lincoln's, Johnson's, and Radical Republicans' Reconstruction approaches differ?
  6. In what ways was Reconstruction a turning point, and what inequalities continued?

These are adapted from the six essential questions on the class Period 5 review page.
